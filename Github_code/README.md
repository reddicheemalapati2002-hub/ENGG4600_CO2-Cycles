## What changed from the interim/conference version (summary)
* Recuperator limited by both streams (second-law fix) → CO₂ efficiencies
  fall 3–11 pp; no sCO₂/SRC crossover; tCO₂ no longer beats steam.
* Heater gas profile linear in heat duty → double pinch at both ends, not a
  mid-exchanger pinch; LMTD +129–223 % result unchanged.
* One consistent heater-sizing and cost method for all three cycles
  (Weiland 2019, Turton, DOE benchmarks), condenser included.
* Economics on a fixed-source basis (plant sized to what each cycle recovers
  from the stream), discounted payback, breakeven analysis.
* New Australian heat-sink findings: tCO₂ at 14 °C condensing needs a
  chiller that would consume 63–134 % of net output; sCO₂ loses 42 % of its
  200 °C efficiency between 32 and 40 °C compressor inlet.
* Conclusion: steam Rankine is the default above 300 °C; tCO₂ only where a
  natural cold sink exists; simple recuperated sCO₂ not viable below 500 °C
  in Australia.
